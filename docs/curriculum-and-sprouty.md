# Curriculum and Sprouty

The current library contains **151 lessons across 35 modules**, including 23 new modules with 92 lessons. Existing lesson IDs and prior completion records remain valid.

New subjects include financial goals, budgeting, emergency savings, banking, interest, credit, debt repayment, asset classes, allocation, taxes, retirement, insurance, scams, financial security, research, fundamentals, macroeconomics, execution, strategies, testing, leverage, options, futures, forex, digital assets, psychology, and practice plans.

Dedicated modules cover stock research, bullish/bearish scenarios, and public insider disclosures. Insider lessons explain SEC Forms 3/4/5, transaction codes, indirect holdings, footnotes, and prearranged trading plans. They distinguish public disclosures from material nonpublic information, and do not treat purchases or sales as certain price signals. Sources link to official SEC, IRS, or Investor.gov resources as appropriate.

Each lesson has an explanation, example, practical purpose, and knowledge check. First correct completion awards 35 XP; full module completion awards another 100 XP once. Each added module has a mastery badge; broader milestones recognize 50 lessons, 100 lessons, and the completed current curriculum. Rewards indicate learning progress, not certification or guaranteed investment skill. Search and topic filters keep the enlarged library navigable.

The starting message distinguishes learning from live trading: there is no universal starting amount, and learning and virtual practice can begin now. Actual accounts and products may have minimums, and real-money readiness depends on individual finances, goals, and knowledge.

Sprouty uses an original mouthless, limbless leaf design and restrained movement, disabled for reduced-motion preferences. Four growth stages use fixed completed-lesson thresholds (0, 1, 10, 30), so adding more curriculum does not shrink previously earned growth. Sprouty appears in onboarding, lessons, quiz feedback, trade explanations, milestones, and loading screens. Trade feedback invites review without claiming an unverified entry was good.

The always-available help chat uses local, deterministic retrieval over Sprout support information and the lesson library. It is **automated library-based help, not a generative model or live human support**. It requires no external AI service or paid API key. Answers include internal sources; unsupported questions get a limitation statement rather than an invented answer. It cannot access credentials, change account records, fetch live insider information, or make personalized trade recommendations. Chats stay in the current page session and are not uploaded or stored in the account.

## Presentations and quizzes

Every lesson now has a three-part presentation before its quiz. A module presentation covers each of its lessons, followed by a review quiz. Quiz length is validated to stay within 1–25 questions; chart topics include an illustrated decision scenario. The passing score is 80%, answers lock when checked, and failed attempts offer review and retry. Completion rewards and the Continue button appear only after a passing result is saved. Existing completed lessons retain their progress and one-time XP.

Completion screens distinguish lesson and module completion, show the quiz score and XP earned, and offer the next lesson/module, Simulator, or Dashboard. Sprouty offers calm encouragement. Simulator research cards suggest stocks and ETFs for educational comparison, exclude examples already held, and never place orders automatically or predict returns.

## Discovery and personal paths

The dashboard adds a daily rotating annotated practice chart (New York calendar day, explicitly illustrative), a four-question education preference quiz, completed lesson and badge counts, missed-answer review links, and tool shortcuts. Preferences persist locally in the same browser; they are not synced to an account. A missed quiz answer is recorded even when an overall module score passes. Learning adds a searchable 20-term glossary with worked examples and tutorial links plus five chart-based common mistake scenarios. The homepage uses the requested confidence headline and the roadmap adds Strategy Labs. Market snapshot and practice statistics continue using actual simulator data, rather than preset example results.

## Focused navigation and five-question assessments

Learn now opens directly to Modules & lessons, shows six modules initially, and nests expandable lesson lists inside their module cards. Topic filters, module/lesson search, and Show more reveal additional content on demand. Video tutorials and Glossary & habits have separate sections; chart practice is housed in Practice Lab. Practice Lab separates Chart lab, Journal, Challenges, Results, and Mindset. Sidebar links are grouped into Overview, Learn & practice, Trading workspace, Community & rewards, and Account. Dashboard removes repeated roadmap and promotional blocks; referrals remain in Profile and mission/benefits remain on the homepage.

Every one of the 151 lesson assessments and 35 module assessments contains exactly five questions. Lesson quizzes cover the idea, its worked example, why it matters, interpreting assumptions, and a module-specific application scenario. Module quizzes review four module concepts and one application scenario. Presentations introduce the application scenario and assumptions before the quiz. Four correct answers (80%) are required to pass. Existing saved progress, rewards, and completion animations are preserved.

Learning mode is saved in the profile: Beginner presents clear foundations and recall/application checks; Intermediate adds connections within the module and applied reasoning; Advanced adds deeper examples and critical scenario/source analysis. Each mode retains exactly five questions and the 80% pass threshold. Existing profiles default from their onboarding experience, or Beginner when unspecified.
